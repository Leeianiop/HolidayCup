December 3rd, one last flick

The HIFA Holiday Cup was created in November 2025 after the great highlighter depression.

It was proposed as a “super-cup” to wrap up the season and celebrate the upcoming holiday breaks

The format of the tourney features different varieties, from 1v1v1v1 down to a 1v1.

The Holiday Cup is well-known in the history of HIFA for producing iconic moments and kills.

As of now GoofyYellow remains the standing champion of the tourney, after giving us an incredible final back in December 5th 2025.

The HIFA Holiday Cup remains a tradition, becoming the largest HIFA tourney and a celebration of the game.

Participation is available for students in F4/J3 only

16 players face off in 4 games of 1v1v1v1, gaining one point per kill in each round

The top 8 qualifiers join the 4 auto-qualifiers to form 4 groups of 3

The 8 remaining face off in knockouts advancing from quarterfinals to La Finale
